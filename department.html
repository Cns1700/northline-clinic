Primary Care

The first stop for most patients on Springhill Avenue. Annual exams, chronic-condition follow-up, and same-week sick visits for adults and older teens.

Primary Care at Northline Clinic is built around a panel, not a waiting-room lottery. When you join the practice you are assigned a physician or physician assistant and a charge nurse. Those are the people who know your chart, your medications, and which specialist down the hall already has your last set of labs.

Dr. Amara Whitfield, the clinic’s medical director, still sees patients two full clinic days each week. Dr. Luis Ortega covers family medicine and older-adult visits. Elena Brooks, RN, coordinates referrals, vaccine clinics, and same-day sick slots so a cough or a blood-pressure spike does not wait for next month’s physical.

Cardiology, orthopedics, women’s health, and behavioral health sit in the same building. A referral rarely means leaving 88 Springhill Ave. Walk-ins are welcome for urgent concerns during weekday hours. For records, portal passwords, or IT help, use the request form instead of calling the front desk.
